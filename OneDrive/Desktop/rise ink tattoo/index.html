About

Art rooted in Surat.

Rise Ink started as a single-chair studio with one goal: give Surat clients work that stands up to anything you see from studios in Mumbai or abroad. No shortcuts on needle quality, no rushing a session to fit more bookings in.

Fenil draws from Indian devotional art, Japanese linework, and contemporary realism. The result is a style that feels personal rather than generic.
